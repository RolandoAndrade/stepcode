## Decisions worth remembering

- Builtin spellings are reserved words at the lexer level (`longitud` → the `Longitud` builtin).
  The checker should diagnose `Definir` of a builtin name with a rename hint.
- Identifiers `y`, `o`, `si`, `no` collide with es keywords; `Función` folds to `Funcion`.
- Semicolons: required after simple statements only; never after block openers/closers.
- Array types: bracket shorthand only (`Entero[]`, `Entero[,]`, `Entero[3,3]`); `Arreglo De` withdrawn.
- `$ arrays@stepcode` dropped; `indexBase` is a profile option.
- Extra nodes are kept, never dropped: second `Proceso` → `Program.extraMains`; second
  `De Otro Modo` merges into `otherwise`; a nested subprogram (E2015) stays a statement of its
  block (`SubprogramDecl.misplaced`) and is also referenced from `Program.subprograms`.
- Lexer `error` tokens are skipped silently by the parser (one mistake, one diagnostic).
- Diagnostics are returned sorted by `span.start` (stable; lexer before parser at equal offsets).
